SHOPASSIST RETURN POLICY (version 7)

RETURN WINDOW
Items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date shown on the order. The delivery date is the date the carrier recorded the delivery, not the date of the purchase. Items delivered more than 30 days ago can no longer be returned under the standard policy, but a customer may ask a human agent to review a late return when there is a documented reason, such as a hospital stay or a carrier error.

CONDITION OF RETURNED ITEMS
Returned items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging, with all tags attached. Shoes must be returned in the original shoe box, and the shoe box must be placed inside a shipping box. Items that show signs of wear, stains, odours or alterations are not accepted and are sent back to the customer at the store's expense the first time and at the customer's expense the second time.

ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED
The following items cannot be returned for hygiene or legal reasons: underwear, swimwear with the hygiene strip removed, earrings, opened cosmetics, gift cards, downloadable products and personalised items such as engraved jewellery or printed shirts. Sale items marked final sale cannot be returned either, and the final sale label is shown on the product page and on the receipt.

HOW TO START A RETURN
A customer starts a return from the order page by selecting the item and a reason. The store then emails a prepaid return label within one hour. The customer prints the label, attaches it to the parcel and drops the parcel at any partner carrier point. The return is registered when the carrier scans the parcel, and the customer receives a confirmation email with a return number that starts with RET.

REFUND METHOD AND TIMING
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds appear within 5 to 10 business days after the warehouse has inspected the item. Refunds to store credit appear within one business day. Gift card payments are refunded to a new gift card. The original shipping cost is refunded only when the return is caused by a store error, such as a wrong or damaged item.

DAMAGED OR WRONG ITEMS
When an item arrives damaged or the wrong item was delivered, the customer reports it within 7 days of the delivery with a photo of the item and of the parcel. The store sends a replacement at no cost or refunds the order, whichever the customer prefers. The damaged item does not need to be returned unless the store asks for it, which happens for items above 200 in value.

EXCHANGES
Exchanges are handled as a return plus a new order. The customer returns the original item and places a new order for the wanted size or colour. When the new order is placed before the return is registered, the store holds the price of the original order, so the customer never pays a higher price for the same item.

INTERNATIONAL RETURNS
Customers outside the store's home country receive a return label at a reduced rate, and the cost of the label is deducted from the refund unless the return is caused by a store error. Customs duties paid on the original delivery are not refunded by the store, but the customer may claim them back from the customs authority with the return confirmation.

REFUND LIMITS FOR AUTOMATIC PROCESSING
Refunds up to 100 in value are processed automatically by the support assistant once eligibility is confirmed. Refunds above 100 require a human agent, who verifies the order, the payment and the return status before approving. The assistant never promises a refund amount above the automatic limit; it opens a ticket and tells the customer that a human will confirm.

CUSTOMER VERIFICATION
Before any order detail or refund is discussed, the customer is verified by the email address on the order and the order number. The assistant never reads order details to a customer who cannot provide both, and it never sends order details to a third party, including a family member, without the account holder's consent.

PARTIAL RETURNS
A customer may return some items of an order and keep the rest. The refund covers the returned items only. When a promotion required a minimum order value and the kept items fall below that value, the promotion discount is deducted from the refund, and the order confirmation shows the discount that applies.

LOST RETURN PARCELS
When a return parcel is scanned by the carrier but never arrives at the warehouse, the store treats the return as received 14 days after the scan and refunds the customer. When the parcel was never scanned, the customer contacts the carrier point where it was dropped and sends the drop-off receipt to the store, which then refunds the customer.

RETURNS OF GIFTS
A person who received an item as a gift may return it with the gift receipt or the order number. The refund goes to store credit in the name of the person returning the item, never to the buyer's card, so that the buyer is not informed of the return. Gift receipts are valid for 60 days from the delivery date.

WARRANTY CLAIMS
Faults that appear after the 30-day window are handled as warranty claims, not returns. The customer describes the fault, sends photos and receives a repair, a replacement or a refund depending on the item. Warranty claims are reviewed by a human agent within 3 business days, and the assistant only collects the information and opens the claim.

REPEATED RETURNS
Customers who return more than 80 percent of their orders over 12 months are flagged for review. The assistant never mentions the flag to the customer. A human agent reviews the account and may limit the return label service to paid labels, and the customer is informed by email before any limit applies.

CONTACT AND ESCALATION
When the policy does not answer a question, or the customer asks for an exception, the assistant opens a ticket for a human agent and tells the customer that an agent will reply within one business day. The assistant never invents a rule and never promises an outcome that the policy does not state.
